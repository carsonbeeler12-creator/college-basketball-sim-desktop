# College Basketball Dynasty - v0.9.7-beta Devlog

## 🎮 Major Feature: Coaching Schemes System

We're thrilled to announce **v0.9.7-beta**, a major update that brings strategic depth to team management with the all-new **Coaching Schemes System**!

### What Are Schemes?

Schemes are your team's signature playing style that directly influence how your players develop, perform, and interact with your team's philosophy. This isn't just flavor text—schemes have real, measurable impacts on your dynasty.

#### Three Core Schemes:

**1. Pass-Heavy Offense**
- Guards develop faster and reach higher potential
- Centers develop slower
- Forwards have balanced development
- Your team excels in perimeter shooting and ball movement
- Perfect for fast-paced, high-scoring basketball

**2. Post-Heavy Offense**
- Centers develop significantly faster and reach higher potential
- Guards develop slower
- Forwards have balanced development
- Your team dominates the paint with strong interior scoring
- Ideal for building around dominant big men

**3. Balanced Offense**
- All positions develop at equal rates
- Maximum flexibility in team building
- No position gets left behind
- Great for dynasties that want variety and adaptability

### How Schemes Impact Your Dynasty

Your chosen scheme affects:
- **Player Development**: Position-specific growth rates that reflect your system
- **Playing Time**: Players develop faster when their position aligns with your scheme
- **Recruiting**: Attract players who fit your system
- **Team Identity**: Build a truly unique dynasty with a distinct playstyle

### Strategic Depth

Schemes add meaningful decisions to dynasty management:
- Do you double down on your scheme with targeted recruiting?
- Do you pivot when key players graduate?
- How do you develop players that don't naturally fit your system?
- Can you build a contender around an unconventional playstyle?
