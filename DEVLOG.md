# What’s new — v0.9.9 beta

*March 2026*

Hey coaches — here’s what **actually changes for you** in this update.

---

### Easier to see your game version
The **Home** screen and the **top bar** now show which build you’re on. Handy if you’re reporting a bug or comparing with a friend.

### Cleaner Home screen
We **removed the “Inject” button** — it was meant for internal testing and confused a lot of people.  

**The 2026 Tournament** is still on Home: one tap loads a fun sandbox bracket with **made-up schools** (not a real NCAA bracket — just something to play with).

### Name your teams (and see it everywhere)
- If you **rename your team** on the Dynasty Hub, that name now shows up **everywhere it should**: standings, rankings, recruiting, sim results, box scores, roster, rotation, and when you’re looking at matchups.
- You can also **rename other schools**: open any team from **Standings** or **Rankings** (team details), tap **✏️**, and change their display name. **Reset** puts the original name back.

### Smarter team ratings & tournament outlook
How strong teams are rated — and labels like tournament outlook — should feel **more in line with your record** and who you’ve played, with less weird “everyone’s a bubble team” stuff.

### Games feel a bit more alive
Simmed games have **a bit more upset potential** and **more variety** in how teams shoot and perform from night to night. Takeover / “hero” moments are toned down so games don’t always swing the same way.

### Prestige when you have a rough year
A **losing season** still costs you, but the hit is **slightly less harsh** than before — one bad year shouldn’t tank your program quite as hard.

### Recruiting tweaks
Behind the scenes we adjusted how recruiting **progress and battles** work, and how **gems and busts** show up, so the board should feel **fairer and less same-y** from class to class.

---

Thanks for playing the beta — every save and every bit of feedback helps. **Go win one.**
