## Core Seeding Formula

```
STEP 1: Resume Score (for at-large selection)
────────────────────────────────────────────
resumeScore = (winPct × 0.55) + (confWinPct × 0.25) + (rating × 0.20)

Example: 25-8 team with 14-6 conf record, prestige 65
→ (25/33 × 0.55) + (14/20 × 0.25) + (0.65 × 0.20)
→ (0.758 × 0.55) + (0.700 × 0.25) + (0.13)
→ 0.417 + 0.175 + 0.13 = 0.722

STEP 2: Seed Score (for final seeding)
───────────────────────────────────────
seedScore = (rating × 0.50) + (resumeScore × 0.50)

Example with above resume:
→ (0.65 × 0.50) + (0.722 × 0.50)
→ 0.325 + 0.361 = 0.686

STEP 3: Place in Bracket
────────────────────────
Sort all 64 teams by seedScore (highest = best)
Use S-curve to distribute into 4 regions:
  Seed #1 → South, West, Midwest, East (one per region)
  Seed #2 → East, Midwest, West, South (reversed)
  Seed #3 → South, West, Midwest, East (reversed)
  ...continue alternating...
```

## Resume Score Weighting Breakdown

```
Component          Weight    Description
─────────────────  ────────  ──────────────────────────────────
Overall Record     55% (0.55) Wins / (Wins + Losses)
Conference Record  25% (0.25) Conf Wins / (Conf Wins + Conf Losses)
Team Rating        20% (0.20) Prestige / 100 (or teamRating if set)
                   ────────
                   100% = Final Resume Score

Why this weighting:
  • Overall record = primary indicator (55%)
  • Conference record = secondary indicator (25%)
  • Team prestige = slight boost for established programs (20%)

What's missing:
  ✗ Point differential (shows dominance)
  ✗ Quality wins (beating good teams)
  ✗ Strength of schedule (toughness of opponents)
  ✗ Head-to-head (direct matchups)
```

## Expected Tournament Outcomes

### Typical 64-Team Tournament
- **32 autobids** - Conference champions (mostly)
- **32 at-large** - Best remaining teams
- **4 regions** - East, West, South, Midwest
- **16 seeds** - Each region has #1 through #16 seed
- **63 games** - 4 rounds to championship

### Who Usually Gets In
1. **Automatic:** Every conference champion (~32)
2. **At-Large:** 
   - All 6-7 Power conference tournament runners-up
   - Strong mid-major champions (if good enough record)
   - Best non-autobid teams below that

### Who Usually Gets Left Out
- Teams with losing records (even good teams)
- Teams from weak conferences without tournament
- Teams that lost conference tournament final

## Seeding Reality Check

### How S-Curve Works
```
Looking at Seed #1 spots:
Without S-Curve:
  Team A (seed score 0.90) → South
  Team B (seed score 0.89) → South
  Team C (seed score 0.88) → South
  Team D (seed score 0.87) → South
  ❌ All #1 seeds in one region!

With S-Curve (alternating):
  Team A (seed score 0.90) → South
  Team D (seed score 0.87) → East
  Team B (seed score 0.89) → West
  Team C (seed score 0.88) → Midwest
  ✅ Balanced across regions!
```

## Missing Metrics Explained

### RPI (Rating Percentage Index)
```
What it is:
  25% × Your Win%
  50% × Opponent Average Win%
  25% × Opponent's Opponent Average Win%

Why it matters:
  Beating a 30-win team = much better than 10-win team
  
Example:
  Team A: 25-8 (0.758)
  Opponent avg: 0.550
  Opp's opp avg: 0.500
  RPI = (0.25 × 0.758) + (0.50 × 0.550) + (0.25 × 0.500)
      = 0.190 + 0.275 + 0.125
      = 0.590 (out of 1.0)
```

### Strength of Schedule (SOS)
```
What it is:
  Average of all opponents' win percentage
  
Why it matters:
  30-3 vs weak teams ≠ 30-3 vs Duke/Kansas
  
Example:
  Team A opponents: 25-8, 22-10, 20-12, etc.
  Average: 0.65 win%
  
  vs.
  
  Team B opponents: 15-18, 12-21, 10-23, etc.
  Average: 0.40 win%
  
  Same 30-3 record but Team A's is worth more
```
